3. IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE
========================================================

DO NOT overclaim causality.

If vegetation or water indicators increase after an intervention, do NOT automatically state:

“The intervention caused the improvement.”

Instead use:

“Observed spatial change”

“Observed indicator trend”

“Detected change”

“Provisional observation”

“Requires contextual validation”

The system should consider that observed changes may also be influenced by:

- rainfall
- seasonality
- land-use changes
- nearby interventions
- environmental conditions
- data quality

This principle must appear in the relevant analytics and report sections.
